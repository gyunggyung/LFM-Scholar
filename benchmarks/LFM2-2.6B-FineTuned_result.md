# Related Work

Transformer models have revolutionized natural language processing (NLP) by enabling state-of-the-art performance across a wide range of tasks. Below is a thematic grouping of relevant literature:

## 1. **State-of-the-Art Transformer Models**
- [patro2024mamba360] Mamba-360: Survey of State Space Models as Transformer Alternative for Long Sequence Modelling
- [rahali2023endtoend] End-to-End Transformer-Based Models in Textual-Based NLP
- [zayyanu2024revolutionising] Revolutionising Translation Technology: A Comparative Study of Variant Transformer Models - BERT, GPT, and T5

## 2. **Robustness and Security**
- [neerudu2023robustness] Impact of Transformer-Based Models in NLP: An In-Depth Study on BERT and GPT
- [liu2022piccolo] Piccolo: Exposing Complex Backdoors in NLP Transformer Models
- [zeng2024clibe] CLIBE: Detecting Dynamic Backdoors in Transformer-based NLP Models

## 3. **Applications in Domain-Specific Tasks**
- [jacobs2024system] System Optimizations for Enabling Training of Extreme Long Sequence Transformer Models
- [pasch2022nlp] NLP for Responsible Finance: Fine-Tuning Transformer-Based Models for ESG
- [raffel2019exploring] Exploring the Limits of Transfer Learning with a Unified Text-to-Text Transformer
- [gillioz2020overview] Overview of the Transformer-based Models for NLP Tasks

## 4. **Efficiency and Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning**
- [liu2021exploring] On Exploring Attention-based Explanation for Transformer Models in Text Classification
- [campino2024unleashing] Unleashing the Transformers: NLP Models Detect AI Writing in Education
- [vasileiou2024explaining] Explaining Text Similarity in Transformer Models

## 5. **Evaluation and Benchmarking**
- [ezheng2019benchmark] A Benchmark for Evaluating the Performance of Transformer-based Models on NLP Tasks
- [raffel2019exploring] On Exploring the Limits of Transfer Learning with a Unified Text-to-Text Transformer

## 6. **Emerging Trends**
- [zhang2023applications] Applications of Transformer-based Language Models in Bioinformatics: A Survey
- [ezhang2024review] Parameter-Efficient Transfer Learning for NLP

This review highlights the transformative impact of transformer models across NLP, addressing challenges such as robustness, efficiency, and domain adaptation. Future work will focus on improving model interpretability and scalability.
