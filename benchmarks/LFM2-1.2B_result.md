# Related Work

**Related Work**

**Theme 1: State-of-the-art Augmented NLP Transformer Models for Direct and Single-step Retrosynthesis**
This section focuses on the development of transformer-based models specifically designed to predict chemical retrosynthesis pathways, a task that requires understanding and generating molecular structures from textual descriptions.

- **[tetko2020stateoftheart] State-of-the-art augmented NLP transformer models for direct and single-step retrosynthesis**: This paper investigates how data augmentation can enhance the performance of transformer models in retrosynthesis tasks. It shows that augmenting training data with synthetic SMILES sequences improves the model's ability to predict chemical reactions directly, outperforming traditional methods [1].

**Theme 2: Recognition and Prediction of Surgical Gestures and Trajectories Using Transformer Models in Robot-Assisted Surgery**
While not directly related to retrosynthesis, this section explores the application of transformer models in robotic surgery, highlighting their potential for improving surgical precision and safety through gesture recognition.

- **[shi2022recognition] Recognition and Prediction of Surgical Gestures and Trajectories Using Transformer Models in Robot-Assisted Surgery**: This study demonstrates how transformer architectures can be adapted to recognize and predict surgical gestures and trajectories, contributing to more intuitive and controlled robotic-assisted surgical procedures [2].

**Theme 3: Transformers in NLP**
This section provides a comprehensive overview of the transformative impact of transformer models across various NLP tasks, from text classification to machine translation.

- **[salıcı2024impact] Impact of Transformer-Based Models in NLP**: This paper reviews the significant advancements brought about by transformer models in NLP, including their ability to handle long-range dependencies and contextual understanding, which has revolutionized the field [3].

**Theme 3.5: Exploring Attention-based Explanation for Transformer Models in Text Classification**
This section delves into the development of attention mechanisms within transformers to provide explanations for model predictions, enhancing transparency and trust in NLP systems.

- **[zayyanu2024revolutionising] Explaining Text Similarity in Transformer Models**: This study focuses on generating explanations for text similarity tasks using transformer models, providing insights into how these models reason about textual content [4].

**Theme 3.6: Parameter-Efficient Transfer Learning for NLP**
This section discusses techniques that enable efficient fine-tuning of large transformer models, addressing the challenge of parameter inefficiency in multi-task learning scenarios.

- **[houlsby2019parameterefficient] On Robustness of Finetuned Transformer-based NLP Models**: While primarily focused on model robustness, this work also touches on the efficiency of fine-tuning transformer models for various NLP tasks, including those requiring extensive parameter adjustments [5].

**Theme 3.7: Piccolo: Exposing Complex Backdoors in NLP Transformer Models**
This section addresses the vulnerability of transformer models to backdoor attacks, where subtle modifications can lead to unintended behaviors, emphasizing the need for robustness and security in NLP applications [6].

**Theme 3.8: CLIBE: Detecting Dynamic Backdoors in Transformer-based NLP Models**
This work introduces a method to detect dynamic backdoors in transformer models, which are triggered by specific input features, highlighting ongoing research into securing these powerful models against adversarial manipulations [7].

Each of these sections provides a structured overview of the related work, grouped by thematic clusters, ensuring that the contributions of each paper are clearly contextualized within the broader field of NLP and transformer models.
