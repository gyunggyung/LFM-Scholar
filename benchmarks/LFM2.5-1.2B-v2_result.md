Deep learning has emerged as a powerful tool for time-series forecasting, offering significant improvements over traditional statistical methods. This section reviews the state-of-the-art deep learning models applied to meteorological element forecasting, including LSTM, GRU, and attention-based architectures.

\subsection{Time-Series Forecasting of Chlorophyll-a in Coastal Areas}
Recent studies have explored various deep learning models for predicting chlorophyll-a concentrations in coastal waters. These models leverage the temporal dependencies in the data to capture complex patterns and improve prediction accuracy.
